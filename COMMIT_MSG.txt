Subagent audit fixes: CastleThreat, per-door squad counts, choice wedge

CastleThreat no longer falls back to the nearest enemy to hero - it is now
strictly the nearest foe to the castle, so HasCastleThreat correctly gates
night coin scavenging and red-alert checks.

Squad posting uses per-door DoorPostCounts/DoorPostAts arrays instead of a
global consecutive-line streak. Two alternating uncovered unwalkable doors
used to reset the count forever; now each anchor accumulates its own posts
and ParkDoor fires after 4 failures.

Choice coroutine wedge: if ChoiceCoroutine is running >20 s, the bot now
calls CancelChoice() once and resets the watchdog instead of bouncing
CloseActiveFrame() on a frozen frame.
